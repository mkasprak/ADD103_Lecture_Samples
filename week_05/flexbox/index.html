Day One: Arrival at Mingo Falls

Sparky navigated. Dot packed snacks. After a four-hour drive into the Smoky Mountains, the two ducks checked into their cozy cabin just outside the Qualla Boundary. First stop: Mingo Falls — one of the tallest waterfalls in the Southern Appalachians.

"The mist felt incredible," Dot reportedly quacked. "Sparky just stood there with his hat on looking majestic."

Day Two: History & Fry Bread

The duo visited Oconaluftee Village, a living museum of Eastern Band Cherokee culture. Dot was deeply moved by the traditional basket weaving demonstration. Sparky ate too much fry bread and needed a nap.

Day Three: The Blue Ridge Parkway

Clear skies, 70°F, and mountain views that went on forever. Sparky and Dot drove the Parkway at sunrise, pulling over at every overlook. Dot took 47 photos. Sparky is in none of them — apparently he was "the driver."

?? What Is &amp;?

Notice &amp; in the source code wherever you see Sparky & Dot's name. The & character is reserved in HTML — it signals the start of a special code called an HTML Entity. Writing a bare & can confuse the browser, so we escape it as &amp; to mean “just show an ampersand.”

A few entities you'll use all the time:

  • &amp; → &   (ampersand)
  • &lt; → <   (less-than / opening angle bracket)
  • &gt; → >   (greater-than / closing angle bracket)
  • &copy; → ©   (copyright symbol)
  • &mdash; → —   (em dash — used for pauses)
  • &nbsp; → (non-breaking space — keeps two words glued together)
  • &hellip; → …   (ellipsis)
  • &rarr; → →   (right arrow)
